## Purpose
Build an original Thai-first, mobile-first product that helps ordinary people identify, understand, prevent, and respond to scams and cyber threats. This is not a copy of ScamAdviser and must provide useful evidence and practical next actions, not merely a risk score.

## Product principles
1. Evidence-first: show observable indicators, consulted sources, freshness, and limitations.
2. Clearly separate facts, external reputation signals, and AI inference.
3. Never claim a target is 100% safe; use INSUFFICIENT DATA when evidence is inadequate.
4. Do not make unsupported accusations against people or businesses.
5. Privacy-first: never request or retain passwords, OTPs, cookies, access tokens, or unnecessary personal data.
6. Convert findings into practical next steps.
7. Label synthetic examples as simulations, never real allegations.

## Proposed capabilities
- Universal Threat Checking: public URLs/websites, suspicious message text (SMS/email/chat), screenshots/images, QR codes, social impersonation, and—only after safe handling design—APK/files.
- AI Scam Detective: classify likely patterns and explain indicators, evidence, uncertainty, and safe next steps. AI-only output is not a confirmed malicious verdict.
- Cyber Scam Intelligence Library: 12 initial categories:
  1. Phishing & Fake Website
  2. Romance & Relationship Scam
  3. Shopping & Marketplace Scam
  4. Investment & Crypto Scam
  5. Impersonation & Call Center
  6. Job & Task Scam
  7. AI & Deepfake Scam
  8. Malware & Ransomware
  9. QR & Payment Scam
  10. Identity Theft & Account Takeover
  11. Tech Support & Recovery Scam
  12. Social Media & Charity Scam
- Threat-to-Action report: what was checked, findings, evidence, sources/time, possible pattern, potential exposure, immediate actions, and recovery guidance.
- Supporting concepts: Emergency Rescue Center, sourced Cyber Threat Radar, moderated Community Scam Reports, Online Shop Trust Check, Digital Privacy Checkup, Family Protection, synthetic Scam Simulator, public breach awareness, and Personal Cyber Assistant.

## UX direction

Home Dashboard; Universal Threat Scanner; Scan Result/Evidence Report; AI Scam Detective; Cyber Scam Library; Cyber Threat Radar; Emergency Rescue Center; Search & Scan History; Privacy & Settings.

**Visual direction:** Premium Professional + Hybrid is a proposal only; detailed UX is not yet approved or implemented.

## Proposed roadmap (not locked)
- V1: public URL/site phishing checks, text/image Scam Detective, Scam Library, evidence-based Threat-to-Action report.
- V2: lawful/authorized phone and account checks, moderated community reports, threat radar, emergency rescue.
- V3: safe APK/file analysis, privacy checkup, breach awareness.
- V4: correlated threat intelligence, verified real-time alerts, personal assistant, partner API.
